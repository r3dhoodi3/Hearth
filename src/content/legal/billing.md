## 3. Free trials

Homeowner Plus and {{BRAND}} Pro membership both come with a 3-day free trial for an eligible account (generally, one that has never had that membership before). Your card is required to start the trial, and nothing is charged for the first 3 days.

At the end of the 3 days, we automatically charge the price for the plan you selected, and the subscription then renews on that same schedule until you cancel. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged anything.

You can cancel a trial the same way you cancel a paid membership: from inside the app, in Account > Membership, at any time. We send a reminder before your trial ends so the first charge is never a surprise.

## 4. Automatic renewal disclosure

Before you pay for any {{BRAND}} subscription, we show you the following, next to the button that starts the charge, in plain and readable type:

- That the plan renews automatically.
- The exact price you will be charged.
- How often it renews (weekly, monthly, or every 12 months).
- If a free trial applies, how long it lasts and when the first real charge happens.
- How to cancel, and that you can cancel at any time.
- That your access continues through the end of the period you already paid for, even after you cancel.

This disclosure is shown in the same screen as the payment step, before you enter your billing information, and it is repeated after purchase as a retainable acknowledgment (section 5). It is never hidden behind a link, a tooltip, or a "learn more" click, and nothing on the page argues against it or distracts from it.

These disclosures are designed to satisfy both California's Automatic Renewal Law (Business and Professions Code sections 17600 to 17606) and the federal Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act (15 U.S.C. sections 8401 to 8405), which requires clear disclosure of the material terms, your express informed consent, and a simple way to stop recurring charges.

## 5. How we get your consent

We ask for your affirmative agreement to the automatic renewal terms before any charge happens:

- The renewal disclosure described in section 4 is shown at checkout, in visual proximity to the payment button.
- A checkbox next to that disclosure, unchecked by default, has to be checked before you can proceed to payment. It says: "I agree to the automatic renewal terms above."
- After your subscription starts, we send you an acknowledgment email that repeats the plan, the price, how often it renews, your trial end date if applicable, and a direct link to cancel.
- We keep a record of what you agreed to and when, for at least three years, so we can show what you consented to if there is ever a dispute.

## 6. Cancellation

You can cancel a {{BRAND}} subscription at any time, in the app, in Account > Membership. Cancellation is one click: there is no phone call, no required reason, no retention offer you have to click through, and no penalty.

Cancelling stops the plan from renewing. You keep full access through the end of the period you already paid for; we do not cut off access early and we do not owe you a partial refund for the unused days, except as described in section 8.

You can also manage or cancel your subscription through the Stripe billing portal, which we link to from the same Account > Membership page, if you would rather do it that way.

If for any reason you cannot cancel in the app, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will process the cancellation within 3 business days.

## 7. Renewal reminders and price changes

If you are on a yearly plan, we send you a reminder between 15 and 45 days before your plan renews, so you have real time to decide whether to keep it. Every active subscriber, on any billing cycle, also gets at least one reminder a year that their membership renews automatically, even if nothing about the price or plan is changing.

If we ever raise the price on a plan you already subscribed to, we will tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price takes effect. The higher price only applies starting at your next renewal after that notice, never retroactively and never mid-period. You can cancel any time before the new price takes effect, using the same one-click cancellation described in section 6.

## 8. Refunds

Subscription charges are not refunded for partial periods. If you cancel partway through a week, month, or year you already paid for, you keep access through the end of that period, but we do not refund the unused portion.

We will refund a subscription charge in these situations:

- You were charged twice for the same period by mistake (a duplicate charge).
- A technical failure on our end prevented you from using the product you paid for.
- A refund is required by law.

To ask for a refund under one of these exceptions, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 14 days of the charge, with the date of the charge and a short description of what happened. We will look into it and respond with a decision.

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} before filing a chargeback with your card issuer. Chargebacks take longer to resolve than a direct refund request and can result in your account being suspended while the dispute is investigated.

## 9. Lead fees and credits for pros

A per-lead fee is charged at the moment you apply to a job, based on the job's tier (section 2). This is a one-time charge, not a recurring subscription.

You get that fee back automatically, as wallet credit, in either of these cases:

- **The homeowner picks someone else.** As soon as the homeowner chooses a different pro for the job, every other pro who paid to apply gets 100% of their fee back as wallet credit. This happens every time, with no limit on how often.
- **Ghost protection.** If the homeowner never responds to any applicant within 7 days of posting the job, every pro who paid to apply on that job gets their fee back as wallet credit. This also happens every time, with no limit.

Wallet credit is not cash. It cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, or transferred to another account or another pro. It can only be applied toward future per-lead fees on your own account. Credit expires 60 days after it is issued if unused. If your account is terminated for violating our terms, any remaining wallet credit is forfeited.

We do not issue cash refunds for lead fees, except where required by law or where we determine, at our discretion, that a platform error caused the charge (for example, a duplicate charge or a technical failure that made the job unreachable).

If you believe a lead was miscategorized, mischarged, or otherwise wrongly billed, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 14 days of the charge with the job ID and a description of the issue, and we will investigate.

Chargebacks on lead fees go through Stripe's normal dispute process. As with subscriptions, please contact us first; wallet credit already issued on a disputed lead is not cash and remains subject to the same expiration and forfeiture rules above.

## 10. Failed payments and past-due accounts

If a renewal charge fails, Stripe automatically retries it over the following days. If the payment continues to fail, your paid access is suspended until you update your payment method, and {{BRAND}} Pro membership perks pause the same way. Reactivation happens automatically once a valid payment method is charged successfully; nothing else needs to happen on your end.
