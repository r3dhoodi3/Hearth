## 1. You are talking to an AI, not a person

Ask {{BRAND}} and the other features listed below are software. No human writes, reviews, or approves an individual answer before you see it. Every answer, cost figure, document read, or drafted message from these features is generated by an artificial intelligence model, specifically Anthropic's Claude, running on Anthropic's paid commercial API. {{BRAND}} does not train its own model and does not fine-tune Claude on your data.

The in-chat disclosure shown at the top of every Ask {{BRAND}} session reads:

> "You're chatting with an AI assistant, not a person."

This line, or one materially like it, is shown every time the chat opens, not just the first time, consistent with California's bot disclosure law (Business and Professions Code sections 17940-17941).

## 2. What Ask {{BRAND}} and related features can do

- Answer questions about home maintenance, home systems, and general upkeep
- Give rough cost ballparks for repairs and replacements
- Suggest maintenance tasks based on your home's systems and age
- Read and summarize an uploaded quote, invoice, or document, and flag things that look missing or unusual
- Draft a first-pass job post, apply message, estimate, or follow-up for you to review and edit before sending
- Pull structured details (systems, ages, warranty dates) out of an uploaded photo or PDF for you to confirm or correct

## 3. What it cannot do

Ask {{BRAND}} and every other AI feature in the app is not, and is not a substitute for:

- A licensed contractor, plumber, electrician, roofer, or HVAC technician
- A licensed home inspector, structural engineer, or other licensed engineer
- An attorney or legal advice of any kind
- A financial advisor, tax professional, or insurance adjuster
- A permit, code inspection, or any government approval
- A firm quote, an appraisal, or a binding offer of any kind

Nothing the AI generates has been reviewed by a licensed professional before it reaches you. It can be confidently wrong, including about prices, safety, code requirements, and what a document says. Treat every AI-generated answer as a draft to check, not a fact to act on without confirming it yourself.

## 4. Safety: stop and call a professional

If you notice any of the following, stop what you are doing and contact a licensed professional, and if it is an emergency, call 911 or your gas or electric utility right away, before relying on anything Ask {{BRAND}} tells you:

- The smell of gas
- Sparks, burning smells, or exposed wiring
- Water intrusion, active leaks, or flooding
- Any sign of structural damage (cracks, sagging, shifting)

Ask {{BRAND}} may remind you of this, but the responsibility to stop and get real, licensed help in an emergency is yours, not the AI's.

## 5. Cost figures are ballparks, not quotes

Any price, cost range, or estimate that Ask {{BRAND}}, the quote analyzer, or any other {{BRAND}} tool gives you is a rough ballpark for orientation only. The in-app disclaimer shown with these figures reads:

> "{{BRAND}}'s cost figures are ballpark estimates. Confirm with a local pro before you commit."

These figures are not appraisals, not offers, not quotes, and not a promise of what any contractor will actually charge. Get real quotes from licensed pros before you commit to any project.

## 6. What data is sent to Anthropic

When you use an AI feature, your question and relevant context are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. For a homeowner using Ask {{BRAND}}, that can include your first name, property address, city and state, your home systems, up to 30 of your open maintenance tasks, up to 6 of your recent issues, and the text of your question. For a contractor using the pro tools, it can include their own account context, such as license and verification status, wallet balance, and the content of an uploaded quote, invoice, or past-job document.

{{BRAND}} uses the paid, commercial Anthropic API, not a free or consumer product. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train Anthropic's models. Nothing you send to Ask {{BRAND}} is used to build an advertising profile of you, by {{BRAND}} or by Anthropic.

## 7. Where your chat history lives

Ask {{BRAND}} conversation transcripts are not stored on {{BRAND}}'s servers. They are kept only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. If you clear your browser data, switch devices, or use a different browser, your chat history will not follow you. {{BRAND}}'s server stores only usage counters (how many questions you have asked), not the content of your questions or the AI's answers.

## 8. Rate limits

Ask {{BRAND}} has daily and short-burst usage limits, because each question has a real cost to us at the AI vendor. Free accounts get a limited number of questions per day; {{BRAND}} Plus and {{BRAND}} Pro members get higher limits. When you hit a limit, the assistant tells you so rather than failing silently.

## 10. Other automated features

{{BRAND}} also uses smaller, non-AI-chat automated tools behind the scenes:

- **Fraud and risk scoring:** a rules-based system (not machine learning) that looks at salted, one-way hashes of signals like device, IP address, phone number, parcel, and email. It flags suspicious activity for review; by default it does not automatically block a purchase.
- **Lead tiering:** an automated system that sorts posted jobs by trade and likely value so contractors see relevant leads.
- **Document field extraction:** automatically pulls structured fields (like a system type or a warranty date) out of an uploaded document or photo for you to confirm or correct before anything is saved to your account.

None of these automated systems makes a final decision that denies you service, access, or a purchase without a path to human review. If any automated decision affects your account, you can always reach a person by writing to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
